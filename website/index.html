Photo grid

Collages that hold their shape

A grid is a partition of the canvas, not a separate kind of document - so a cell is just a window onto the same editor. Cut out a subject, stack a filter, drop in text: every tool works inside a cell exactly as it does anywhere else.

  • 🧩

    18 layouts, 2 to 5 photos

    Four ways to place two photos, four for three, five each for four and five - named the way you'd describe them: Side by side, Big left, 2 over 3. Swap layout at the same count and photo N stays in cell N.

  • ↔️

    A divider moves two cells

    Drag one and it trades space between exactly those two neighbours - every other cell keeps its rect. No cell shrinks past 12% of its split, and the whole drag is a single undo.

  • 🖼️

    The border is the background

    Gaps and the outer frame are the fill showing through, not a stroke laid over your photos. One Border slider from 0 to 80 px sets both, Corners runs 0 to 60 px, and there are nine preset colours.

  • 🔓

    Nothing is locked in

    Count, layout, border, corners and colour stay in the Grid panel for the life of the project. Dropping to fewer photos does remove the ones in the cells that go - and one undo brings them back.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Background removal, object erase and every other edit run on your device. A photo only leaves your phone if you explicitly share or export it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is it really free?

Yes - all editing tools are free and supported by ads. If you'd rather not see ads, a single one-time in-app purchase removes them permanently.

Does the AI need an internet connection?

No. The AI models run locally, so cut-outs and object removal work fully offline. A connection is only used to load ads.

Are the filters on this page the real ones?

Yes. The 15 looks in the gallery above are the app's own colour matrices, exported from the source and applied by your browser - not screenshots or approximations. A build step regenerates them from the code, and a test renders every look both ways and compares the pixels, so the page can't drift from the app.

Can I change a collage's layout after the photos are in?

Yes. Photo count, layout, border width, corner radius and border colour all stay in the Grid panel for the life of the project. Swapping to another layout at the same photo count keeps each photo in its cell and re-fits it to the new shape. Dropping to a lower count removes the photos in the cells that go, as one undoable step.

Does it work on a tablet, or in landscape?

Both. Turn the phone and the tool dock becomes a rail down the left edge with a folding panel beside it, so the photo gets the width. Tablets get the same layout with a larger canvas.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer. Object removal works best on devices with a bit more RAM; background removal runs everywhere.

Is there an iPhone version?

Chromis is Android-first. An iOS version may follow - let us know if you'd like it.
