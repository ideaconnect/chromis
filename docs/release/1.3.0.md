# Chromis 1.3.0 (build 6)

Previous release: 1.2.0+5. A **minor** bump: this release adds features
(generative fill, snapping, placement sliders for any layer), so semver says
1.3.0. Bumped in `pubspec.yaml` - Play rejects an upload that reuses a
versionCode, so the `+6` is not optional.

## Play Console "What's new"

Paste `whatsnew-1.3.0.txt` verbatim. It is in Play's multi-language block
format - `<en-US>…</en-US>`, one block per listed language - and the 500
character cap is **per language, not per file**. Longest is en-US at 444
counting the newlines twice, which is what a paste that converts LF to CRLF
costs. `python tool/check_store_listings.py` now measures this; it used to
measure only the title, short and long description.

## What is in it

**Object removal fills the background back in.** "Remove object" now leads with
a Fill in / Erase chooser and **Fill is the default**, because "remove object"
means a closed background to nearly everyone who taps it - a transparent hole
read as a broken fill often enough to be the bug report that changed it. Fill
has two tiers tried in order: bundled MI-GAN (26.7 MB, MIT), then a pure-Dart
content-aware fill that always runs. The tier is invisible on purpose; it is
not a mode anyone picks.

**Snapping.** A moved layer is taken to the nearest alignment - leading edge,
centre and trailing edge on each axis - against every other visible layer and
against the canvas, with a magenta guide drawn on whatever it caught. It works
in a *frame*, so two layers at the same angle can be made genuinely flush
rather than merely bounding-box adjacent. The toggle sits in Adjust, governs
both the canvas gesture and the placement sliders, and defaults on.

**Placement sliders for any layer.** Scale, Rotation, Horizontal, Vertical and
Opacity now drive a caption or a bubble, not just a photo. That closes a state
with no way out: the canvas hit box IS the layer, so a caption created small -
or pinched down by accident - was smaller than a finger and could not be
grabbed to be made big again. Scale is a curve with unity at a quarter of the
bar, so the shrink range is usable.

**Pure black and pure white.** Every surface that covers the screen is now
`#000000` or `#FFFFFF`. A near-black keeps an AMOLED pixel lit for no reason,
and a 94% white goes muddy on IPS in daylight; structure comes from hairlines
and recessed controls instead.

## Store listing

All six locales rebuilt: `en-US pl-PL de-DE es-ES fr-FR cs-CZ`.

- **`details.md`** - the long description gained the generative fill, snapping,
  placement-slider and light/dark copy, and was trimmed elsewhere to stay under
  Play's 4000. Every locale now lands between 3923 and 3969.
- **Screenshots and feature graphics** were re-captured from scratch, on the
  1.3.0 build, in all six languages, on three devices.
- **The sample photos are new.** They were personal photographs; they are now
  CC0 from Wikimedia Commons, recorded with their licence and source URL in
  `assets/store/samples/SOURCES.json` and fetched by
  `tool/fetch_stock_photos.py`. A Play listing is commercial use of every pixel
  inside the device frame, so which photograph is in it is a decision that
  belongs in the repo rather than in whatever was on the emulator.

The cut-out in the sticker and bubble shots is a **real model output**: made by
running the app's own AI Cut once (`capture_store_shots.py mask phone`) and
keeping the mask it wrote. A hand-drawn alpha would put a cut-out in the
listing that the app did not produce.

## Capture: what had to be fixed

Seeding `proEntitled: true` is not enough to keep ads out, and it fails
quietly. `reconcileEntitlement` re-checks the flag against Play on every launch
and revokes it on a confirmed "not owned" - which is what a Play-store emulator
with no purchase answers. The flag was written, revoked a second after launch,
and the run carried on.

That is not just an ad in a corner. **Without Pro the editor's rail grows a Go
Pro entry at the top, which pushes every tool down one slot**, so the capture
script's tap tables hit Bubble where they meant AI Cut - eight plausible
screenshots of the wrong panels, on both tablets, in every language. The UMP
consent form also came back and covered the whole screen.

`capture_store_shots.py prepare` now disables the Play Store (so
`restorePurchases` throws, and every uncertain case keeps Pro), takes the
radios down, sets the dark theme and sets the flag. Run it once per device
before capturing.

## Build

```bash
flutter build appbundle --release   # -> build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab
```

`android/key.properties` and `secrets/chromis-upload.jks` must be present, or
the signing config silently falls back to **debug keys** and Play refuses the
upload.

## Still open

- The Google Ads image and video assets (`assets/store/ads/`) still carry the
  1.2.0 object-removal wording, which says the segmentation model finds the
  outline and undo brings it back. That is still true, so nothing there is a
  false claim - but it does not mention the fill, and the Polish copy in
  `ADS`/`ADS_BEATS` still has the two grammar faults CLAUDE.md documents
  ("Dotknij obiektu" genitive, and "Cofnięcie go przywraca"). Fixing the text
  means re-rendering those assets, which is a separate pass.
- `editor_screen.dart` is still over 4,000 lines.
