# Title

Chromis: Ultimate Photo Editor

# Short description (up to 80 chars)
Remove backgrounds with on-device AI. Layers, filters, collages. Private.

# Long description (up to 4000 chars)
Chromis is a photo editor whose AI runs on your phone. Cut a subject out of a photo, erase something you did not want in the shot, stack layers, build a collage, and export it. Your photos are not uploaded anywhere.

AI THAT RUNS ON YOUR PHONE
• One tap removes the background and leaves your subject on transparency. Pick between two models: Android's built in segmentation, or an open source U2-Net bundled inside the app.
• Tap an object you want gone: fill the background back in over it, or cut it out to transparency.
• Refine the result: auto clean up stray specks, soften the cut edge, or fix it by hand with an erase and restore brush.
• The U2-Net model ships inside the app, so cut-outs still work with no connection.

LAYERS
• Stack photos, captions and speech bubbles on one canvas.
• Drag to move, pinch to scale and rotate.
• Reorder by dragging, hide, duplicate, rename or delete from the Layers panel.
• 16 blend modes, from Multiply to Luminosity.
• Drop shadow and outline on any layer. On a cut out subject both follow its silhouette rather than a rectangle.
• Set any layer's opacity.
• Merge two layers, or flatten the whole stack into one photo.

COLOUR AND LIGHT
• 14 one tap looks: Vivid, Punch, Chrome, Warm, Cool, Sunset, Dawn, Dusk, Fade, Matte, Retro, Sepia, Mono and Noir, each with a strength slider.
• Brightness, contrast and saturation from 0 to 200 percent, and hue across the full circle.
• HDR that lifts shadows and brings out local detail.
• Vignette with its own size, softness and colour.
• Nothing is baked in. Your original file is never modified, and every value can be moved back.

TEXT AND COMIC BUBBLES
• Captions in five display fonts, or import your own .ttf or .otf file.
• Set size and colour, and give the text an outline with its own thickness and colour.
• Five bubble shapes: Speech, Thought, Shout, Caption and Whisper. Drag the tail to aim it at whoever is talking.

PHOTO GRID COLLAGES
• 2 to 5 photos across 18 layouts.
• Drag a divider and it trades space between exactly those two cells.
• Border up to 80 px, rounded corners up to 60 px, nine frame colours.
• Every tool works inside a cell, so you can cut out a subject in one photo of a collage.
• Shuffle the photos between cells, or drag one onto another to swap them.

PROJECTS
• Bring photos in from your gallery, your camera, or the clipboard.
• Every edit saves itself. Close the app and carry on later.
• Home keeps your recent projects with live previews. Search them by name, rename, duplicate or delete.

CANVAS AND EXPORT
• Start from a photo at its own size, from a preset such as square, portrait or story, or from a size you type.
• Crop freehand, or to 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, 3:2 or 2:3.
• Export PNG, JPG or WebP at 100, 75, 50 or 25 percent of the canvas.
• PNG and WebP keep transparency. Save to your gallery or send straight to another app.
• Undo and redo anything. Projects save themselves while you work.

PRIVACY
• The app asks for one permission: internet, used for ads and purchases.
• No camera, storage or location access. Photos arrive through the Android photo picker, which hands over only the picture you chose.
• No account and no sign in.
• Nothing you edit leaves the device unless you share or export it yourself.

Runs on Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.
